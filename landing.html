01 — Process

Three scans. One edge.

01

Scan

Our scrapers crawl eBay, Kleinanzeigen, and Thomann B-Stock every few hours. Every listing is captured, parsed, and classified by model.

02

Compare

Each listing is matched against Reverb market data. We calculate the real margin — what you'd pay vs. what you'd sell for.

03

Act

Only listings with 20%+ margin surface in the dashboard. Pre-written negotiation messages, direct links, and share tools included.
